# Sameeha's Vehicles™©

A browser-based, two-player point-and-tap game: **You vs AI**. Tap moving vehicles before time runs out to score points. Play in **Easy**, **Medium**, or **Difficult** mode.

**Created by:** Yasir Farooq
**Date of creation:** 23 September 2026

---

## How to Play

1. Open the game page (`index.html`) in any modern browser.
2. Choose a difficulty level — **Easy**, **Medium**, or **Difficult** — on the start screen.
3. Tap **Start game**.
4. Tap moving vehicles on screen to score their points before the AI does. Tapping empty space costs points.
5. A duckling 🦆, a chick 🐥, and a kitten 🐱 appear once every minute — each worth **100 points**.
6. Watch out for collisions between vehicles — they bounce off each other with a sound, but cost no points.
7. Every round lasts **2 minutes**. Whoever has the higher score when time runs out wins.
8. Use **Pause** to stop the game at any time, and **Sound On/Off** to control audio.
9. If you win, enjoy the trophy, the fanfare, and the happy jumping monkey 🐵!

### Difficulty Levels

- **Easy** — Slower vehicles, a slower and less accurate AI opponent.
- **Medium** — Balanced speed, a fair AI opponent playing by the same rules as you.
- **Difficult** — Faster, smaller vehicles, higher penalty for missed taps, and a sharper AI. Designed to be a close, hard-fought match for both sides.

All levels run for the same **2-minute** duration.
